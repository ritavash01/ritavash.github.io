My work

My work focuses on stellar evolution, radio transients, and computational methods in astrophysics.

STELLAR EVOLUTION

PHD RESEARCH · 2025–PRESENT

Jems.jl

I’m developing high-performance, one-dimensional stellar evolution models in Julia at Ghent University, supervised by Prof. Pablo Marchant.

My work explores time-dependent convection, nuclear networks, and computational efficiency, with applications to binary stars and the progenitors of gravitational-wave sources.
